Unenter Edge Shield Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 Applies To: Unenter Edge Shield (UES) & Bot Challenge

1. What Is Unenter Edge Shield?

Unenter Edge Shield (UES) is our proprietary edge defense and bot verification infrastructure. Operating across unenter.live and our distributed zones (including Shop, Tank, Docs, Blog, and API gateways), Edge Shield evaluates incoming network requests to distinguish legitimate human visitors from automated botnets, scraping clusters, credential-stuffing engines, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) threats.

Zero Ad Tracking No Biometrics Client-Side Proof-of-Work RAM-Only Telemetry

Our Core Privacy Commitment: Unlike conventional commercial ad-tech CAPTCHAs, Unenter Edge Shield never tracks your identity across disparate websites, never profiles your personal habits for advertising, and never sells or licenses verification data to third parties.

2. How the Bot Challenge Works

When our edge nodes detect suspicious traffic anomalies (such as high request velocity, anomalous TLS fingerprints, or missing browser features), an interactive or transparent verification challenge is rendered before granting access:

  • 1

    Telemetry & Header Evaluation

    Our edge proxy analyzes request metadata including IP address, HTTP headers, User-Agent, and TLS cipher negotiation (JA3/JA4) to calculate a threat confidence score.

  • 2

    Client-Side Proof-of-Work (PoW)

    Your browser executes a rapid, sandboxed mathematical computation (e.g., finding a SHA-256 hash collision with a targeted difficulty prefix via the native WebCrypto API). This computational cost effectively halts mass automated attacks without impacting human battery or CPU performance.

  • 3

    Browser Environment Sanity Check

    The challenge verifies standard browser rendering properties (e.g. WebGL canvas context, audio context support, screen metrics, and navigator properties) to detect headless automation tools such as Puppeteer, Selenium, or automated curl bots.

  • 4

    Cryptographic Clearance Issuance

    Once the mathematical solution is validated by our edge, an encrypted, signed clearance token is issued to your session cookie, allowing seamless uninterrupted browsing.

3. Information Evaluated & Stored

We believe in strict data minimization. During the challenge execution, the following data points are evaluated:

Network Metadata

IP address, autonomous system number (ASN), geographic country code, and TLS handshake parameters.

Device Environment

Screen resolution, device pixel ratio, color depth, browser engine capabilities, and time zone offset.

Interaction Nonces

Challenge start time, completion duration, and the cryptographic proof-of-work output string.

Clearance Cookie

The encrypted __unt_clearance cookie, containing a signed HMAC timestamp valid for up to 24 hours.

4. What We NEVER Collect or Monitor

To ensure total transparency, Unenter Edge Shield explicitly does NOT:

  • No Cross-Site Profiling: We do not track what you do on other domains, apps, or services.
  • No Personal Identity Extraction: We do not read cookies or storage from other sites, names, social profiles, or financial credentials.
  • No Biometric Recording: We do not track facial geometry, retinal data, voice prints, or continuous mouse/keystroke dynamics across pages.
  • No Ad Brokering: No challenge data is sent to advertising networks or external data brokers.

5. Data Retention & Edge Purging

In-Memory Processing: Challenge solutions and behavioral telemetry are evaluated in volatile edge memory (RAM) and are not written to persistent disk storage during normal verification.

Security Incident Buffering: When an active volumetric DDoS or intrusion attack is detected, aggregated threat telemetry (IP ranges, attack vectors, and hit frequency) is preserved in transient ring buffers for a maximum of 72 hours to configure firewall blocklists, after which it is permanently purged.

Clearance Expiration: The __unt_clearance cookie expires automatically after 24 hours, or immediately if you clear your browser session cookies.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / CCPA)

Under European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and international data protection laws, processing security telemetry is conducted under the legitimate interest of preventing fraud, combating cybersecurity threats, and ensuring platform availability for all users.
