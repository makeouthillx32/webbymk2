Facebook Data Deletion Policy

Instructions and procedures for deleting your personal data associated with Facebook Login on Unenter.

Platform: Unenter Solutions (unenter.live)
Effective Date: September 4, 2026
Policy Status: Active & Meta Compliant

1. Overview & Compliance Mandate

Unenter (unenter.live) supports Facebook Login to provide visitors with a seamless, secure authentication experience across our products, live streams, and community features.

In strict compliance with Meta's Platform Terms (Section 4.f: Data Deletion) and global privacy frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and CPRA), we uphold your absolute right to request the complete, irreversible deletion of all personal data, identifiers, and activity history collected through your Facebook authorization.

Automatic Sync: When you remove Unenter from your Facebook Authorized Apps, our systems receive Meta's Data Deletion Callback and automatically disassociate your account credentials.

2. Method 1: Self-Service Removal via Facebook

You can revoke access and trigger automatic data deletion directly from your Facebook account settings:

  1. 1

    Access Facebook Settings

    Log in to your Facebook profile on web or mobile. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.

  2. 2

    Open Apps & Websites

    In the left sidebar or settings menu, select Apps and Websites to view all services connected to your profile.

  3. 3

    Locate Unenter

    Search for or find Unenter in the list of active applications and click View and Edit or Remove.

  4. 4

    Confirm Deletion

    Click Remove. You may also check the box to request deletion of all interactions or permissions granted to Unenter.

3. Method 2: Direct Purge Request

If you no longer have access to your Facebook profile, or wish to immediately accelerate the permanent purge of all associated records across our databases, submit a direct deletion request:

  1. A

    Email Our Privacy Team

    Send an email to privacy@unenter.live or support@unenter.live with the subject line Facebook Data Deletion Request.

  2. B

    Provide Your Identifiers

    Include the primary email address registered with your account or your Facebook User ID (found in Facebook Apps settings under Unenter).

  3. C

    Direct Dashboard Request

    Alternatively, sign in to unenter.live/dashboard and navigate to Account Settings → Privacy → Delete Account Data.

4. What Data Is Permanently Deleted

Upon receiving an authorized deletion request or Meta callback, our data management pipeline permanently purges:

Facebook Identity Records

Your unique Facebook App-Scoped User ID (ASUID), OAuth tokens, and authorization metadata.

Profile Information

Your full name, registered email address, profile avatar link, and biographical info.

Session & Auth Tokens

All active authentication sessions, browser cookies, refresh tokens, and device identifiers.

Activity & Interaction Logs

Livestream chat history, comments, voting records, and community telemetry.

5. Processing Timeline & Confirmation

Immediate Acknowledgment: Requests submitted via email or callback are registered within 24–48 hours, generating a tracking confirmation code.

Complete Purge: All records are permanently and unrecoverably removed from our production databases, caches, and storage tiers within 30 calendar days.

Statutory Exceptions: Financial records, invoice numbers, and order receipts (if you made a purchase on Shop) are retained strictly as required by applicable tax, commercial, and financial record-keeping laws.
