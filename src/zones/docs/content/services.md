# Unenter Solutions

Everything we've built to run unenter.live is available as a service. You bring the idea — we build it, host it, and keep it alive.

**All payments are handled through [Stripe](https://stripe.com)** — cards, invoices, and subscriptions, fully secured. We never see or store your payment details.

## What we offer

### Custom web apps
A complete, modern web app (Next.js) designed and built for you — landing page, shop, blog, portal, or something nobody's built yet. Delivered live on the internet, not as a zip file.

### Hosting on the platform
Your app runs as a zone on our self-hosted platform: automatic deploys, HTTPS, monitoring, and daily backups included. Your own domain or a `yourname.unenter.live` address.

### Care plans
We keep your app healthy: updates, backups verified monthly, content changes, and a real human to talk to when something needs fixing.

### Self-hosting setup
Want to own your stack the way we do? We set up the same architecture on *your* hardware — containers, deploys, backups, the works — and teach you to run it.
