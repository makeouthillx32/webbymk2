Returns & Exchanges

We offer easy returns and exchanges on all boutique apparel within 30 days.

How to start a return or exchange

  1. Contact us within 30 days with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return or exchange.
  2. Tell us whether you'd like a refund or an exchange (for example, a different size).
  3. We'll reply with instructions for sending the item back.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Ask for an exchange when you contact us and we'll help you swap it, subject to availability.

Questions

Not sure whether an item qualifies? Contact us before sending anything back and we'll sort it out.
