Shipping

Orders from the Unenter boutique ship with USPS. Shipping options and prices are shown at checkout before you pay.

Free shipping over $75

Domestic orders over $75 ship free with Standard Shipping.

Shipping options

  • Standard Shipping (USPS Ground Advantage): estimated 5–7 business days in transit.
  • Priority Mail: estimated 1–3 business days in transit.
  • Priority Mail Express: estimated next business day in transit.

Transit times are USPS estimates from the day your order ships, not guarantees. The options offered for your order and their prices are calculated at checkout from your address and the weight of your order.

Questions about an order

If your order hasn't arrived, arrived damaged, or something else is wrong, contact us with your order number and we'll help.
