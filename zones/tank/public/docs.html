Tank LIVE

About Tank LIVE

The premise of the show is hanging out with the House Members living on 24/7 multi-cam livestreams. Viewers interact directly with the house in real time through audio, chat, and room automation.

Viewers can interact with House Members through SFX (sound effects), TTS (text-to-speech), and even physically affect conditions in the house through Tanktoys. House members and guests deal with non-stop chat interactions, room overrides, and live community chaos.

Cast & Lore

House Members & Couch Surfers

House Members

House Members are the permanent residents living inside the tank on 24/7 surveillance.

Couch Surfers

Couch Surfers are friends, guests, and online anomalies crashing at the house to stir up chaos with the House Members.

Subscriptions

Season Passes

Unlock full chat access, bonus cameras, exclusive cliques, 600 $UNT tokens, and TTS discounts for $60 / 6 months. Season Pass XL provides 2,200 $UNT, TTS priority, and +25% XP multiplier.

In-House Audio

TTS & SFX Playback

Send text-to-speech messages and AI-generated sound effects played across in-room speakers in real-time.

Interactions

Tanktoys & Trinkets

Spend tokens to physically trigger lights, alarms, or climate controls in the house, deploy chat combat items (Shrink Rays, Slime Bombs), or book IRL packages (Tank B&B).

Community

24/7 Live Chat

Converse with fellow Tank Fanatics, scheme with your Clique, earn rank badges, and follow system announcements in real-time.

Progression

XP & Streaks

Gain bonus XP from watch time, active chat, consecutive daily login streaks, and AFK dividend accumulation.

Inventory

Tankems & Crafting

Generate random Tankems for 50 $UNT (or free daily for Season Pass holders) and synthesize compatible items to discover rare new trinkets.

Quests

Chores & Directives

Complete rotating daily chores and directives to earn additional token payouts and account XP.

Factions

Cliques & Alliances

Band together in chat, pool tokens, coordinate house actions, and defend your clique against rivals.
